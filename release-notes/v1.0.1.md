The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

## New things you'll notice

**Looks and layouts**
- New Orbit skin and layout, with its own Glyph icon set.
- Skins and layouts are now separate choices, and each layout has a preview.
- Tabs sit along the top again. Classic is now the sidebar version. The Split button is gone (shift+click a tab to split).
- Popped-out tabs follow the skin and theme you picked.
- The Lebserver skin got a new sign-in look and an ASCII Workouts ring and voice meters.
- A few buttons that did nothing were removed.

**Chat**
- Reply to a message.
- Change your display name: right-click your own name or picture.
- Profile cards show your picture and banner straight away, without flashing the default look first.
- The voice chat is always shown simply as "Voice".

**Watch together**
- Keeps playing in a corner preview when you switch tabs. Corner previews can be dragged and resized.
- Picture-in-picture window for YouTube.
- Drag to reorder the queue, with a thumbnail on each item.
- Play videos from the Vault, or drop a video file on the link box to upload and queue it.
- Leave Watch together without leaving voice.
- Fixed blank pop-out and picture-in-picture windows.
- The voice call keeps going when you switch layout.

**Vault**
- Send Vault photos and videos to chat as a card.
- Tag items with tags and people.
- Automatic albums.

**Phone (web app)**
- Chatward works as a Home Screen app on iPhone, with a Workouts tab.
- Push notifications that show the message text, with an on/off switch in Settings > Notifications.
- One-line message box and a full-width emoji and GIF panel.
- Opens at the newest messages when you come back to it.
- Fixed the top bar looking blurry on iPhone.

## Does the friend's server need updating?

Yes. Several of these (phone app, push notifications, display names, Vault and Watch together changes) need the server updated to this version. Until then the app may say the server runs an older version.
